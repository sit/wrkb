# Ashe

## Roles

ADC

- Health: 630 (+128 per level)
- Health Reg. (5s): 6 (+1 per level)
- Mana: 345 (+30 per level)
- Mana Reg. (5s): 9 (+1 per level)
- Armor: 35 (+4 per level)
- Magic Res.: 30 (+1 per level)
- Move Speed: 335 (+0 per level)
- Attack Dmg.: 60 (+4 per level)
- Attack Spd.: 0.83 (+0.0200 per level)

## Abilities

### [P] Frost Shot

Attacks and damaging abilities slow targets hit by 20% / 22.5% / 25% / 27.5% / 30% for 2 seconds. Attacks deal 100% bonus damage. This damage scales with Critical Rate and Critical Damage at 100% effectiveness. Ashe's Critical Strikes deal no extra damage, instead slowing the target by 40% , decaying over time.

### [1] Ranger's Focus

**Cooldown:** 0.0 / 0.0 / 0.0 seconds

**Cost:** 30.0 / 30.0 / 30.0 / 30.0

Passive : Attacks grant a Focus stack for 4 seconds. At 4 stacks, she can cast this ability Active : Gain 20% / 30% / 40% / 50% Attack Speed for 6 seconds. While active, her attack is transformed into a flurry of arrows, dealing 115% / 120% / 125% / 130% physical damage .

### [2] Volley

**Cooldown:** 15.0 / 12.0 / 9.0 / 6.0 seconds

**Cost:** 65.0 / 60.0 / 55.0 / 50.0

Fires 5 / 7 / 9 / 11 arrows in a cone, dealing 70 / 110 / 150 / 190 ( +100% bonus AD ) physical damage per arrow. Enemies can intercept multiple arrows, but will only take damage from the first. Volley applies a critical slow to enemy champions hit, greatly slowing them.

### [3] Hawkshot

**Cooldown:** 45.0 / 40.0 / 35.0 / 30.0 seconds

Fires a hawk spirit that grants vision of enemies along its path. Re-cast : Explodes the hawk, granting vision of the area around it for 5 seconds. Units caught in the initial explosion are revealed for 5 seconds.

### [4] Enchanted Crystal Arrow

**Cooldown:** 80.0 / 70.0 / 60.0 seconds

**Cost:** 100.0 / 100.0 / 100.0

Launches a crystal arrow of ice that stuns the first champion hit and deals 200 / 350 / 500 ( +40% AP ) magic damage to all nearby enemies. The stun lasts between 1.5 to 3.5 seconds, increasing with the distance of the arrow's flight. Re-cast : Hold and drag to steer the arrow's flight path. The arrow can only deviate from its original launch angle by a fixed amount. Tap the cancel button to stop steering.
