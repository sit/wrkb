- Attack Spd.: 1.00 (+0.0000 per level)
- Crit Chance: 2 (+0 per level)

## Abilities

### [P] Carnivore

Whenever Cho'Gath kills a unit, he recovers 40 - 82 (based on level) Health.

### [1] Rupture

Ruptures the ground at target location, popping enemy units into the air, dealing damage and slowing them.

### [2] Feral Scream

Cho'Gath unleashes a terrible scream at enemies in a cone, dealing magic damage and Silencing enemies for a few seconds.

### [3] Vorpal Spikes

Cho'gath's attacks passively release deadly spines, dealing damage to all enemy units in front of him.

### [4] Feast

Devours an enemy unit, killng champions at low health and most minions instantly.  Every successful Feast increases Cho'Gath's health. Maximum 6 growths.
