# Skarner

Jungle

## Base Stats

- Health: 690 (+128 per level)
- Health Reg. (5s): 9 (+1 per level)
- Mana: 380 (+37 per level)
- Mana Reg. (5s): 12 (+1 per level)
- Armor: 46 (+4 per level)
- Magic Res.: 40 (+2 per level)
- Move Speed: 350 (+0 per level)
- Attack Dmg.: 62 (+4 per level)
- Attack Spd.: 0.80 (+0.0150 per level)

## Abilities

### [P] Threads of Vibration

Skarner's attacks, Shattered Earth , Upheaval , and Impale apply Quaking to targets for 4 seconds (max 3 stacks) . At 3 stacks of Quaking , enemies take 5% - 10% of their max Health as magic damage over its duration. Deals up to 100 - 450 damage against monsters.

### [1] Shattered Earth

**Cooldown:** 7.0 / 5.0 / 4.0 / 3.0 seconds

**Cost:** 45.0 / 45.0 / 45.0 / 45.0

First Cast : Grabs a boulder, empowering his next 3 attacks to gain 25% / 30% / 35% / 40% Attack Speed and deal 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 ( +100% bonus AD +4% bonus Health ) physical damage to nearby enemies. The third attack deals an additional 10% of the target's max Health as physical damage and slows by 45% for 1 second. Recast (Upheaval) : Ends this ability and throws the boulder in a target direction. The boulder explodes on the first enemy hit, dealing 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 ( +100% bonus AD +4% bonus Health ) + 9% of the target's max Health as physical damage to them and nearby enemies and slowing them by 40% for 1 second. Percent Health damage to monsters from the first attack and Upheaval is capped at 200 / 250 / 300 / 350 ( +100% bonus AD +4% bonus Health ). The first two attacks deal 100% damage to structures. The third attack and Upheaval deal 200% damage to structures.

### [2] Seismic Bastion

**Cooldown:** 9.0 / 8.0 / 7.0 / 6.0 seconds

**Cost:** 60.0 / 65.0 / 70.0 / 75.0

Immediately gains a shield that absorbs damage equal to 8% max Health for 2.5 seconds and triggers an earthquake that deals 50 / 70 / 90 / 110 ( +80% AP ) magic damage and slows nearby enemies by 25% for 1 second.

### [3] Ixtal's Impact

**Cooldown:** 20.0 / 18.0 / 16.0 / 14.0 seconds

**Cost:** 50.0 / 55.0 / 60.0 / 65.0

First cast : charges in a target direction, ignoring terrain; If he runs into an enemy champion or large monster, he drags them along for the rest of his charge; Colliding into a wall with an enemy in tow deals 60 / 90 / 120 / 150 ( +120% bonus AD +6% max Health ) physical damage to the enemy and stuns them for 1.5 seconds. Recast : Ends his charge early. Colliding into a wall with an enemy in tow reduces Ixtal's Impact's cooldown by 35% .

### [4] Impale

**Cooldown:** 80.0 / 70.0 / 60.0 seconds

**Cost:** 100.0 / 100.0 / 100.0

Lashes his tails forward, dealing 150 / 250 / 350 ( +100% AP ) magic damage and s uppressing the first 3 enemy champions he hits for 1.5 seconds. Champions hit are dragged along by Skarner for the duration of the suppression . If he hits at least one enemy champion: He gains 40% Movement Speed for 1.5 seconds; If Shattered Earth is active, he will cast Upheaval first.
