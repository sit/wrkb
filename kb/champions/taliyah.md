# Taliyah

## Roles

Mid Lane, Jungle

- Health: 630 (+89 per level)
- Health Reg. (5s): 8 (+0 per level)
- Mana: 460 (+37 per level)
- Mana Reg. (5s): 12 (+1 per level)
- Armor: 34 (+4 per level)
- Magic Res.: 36 (+1 per level)
- Move Speed: 350 (+0 per level)
- Attack Dmg.: 54 (+3 per level)
- Attack Spd.: 0.75 (+0.0136 per level)

## Abilities

### [P] Rock Surfing

Gains 5% / 10% / 20% / 35% Movement Speed while out of combat and near a wall.

### [1] Threaded Volley

**Cooldown:** 6.0 / 5.0 / 4.0 / 3.0 seconds

**Cost:** 60.0 / 60.0 / 60.0 / 60.0

Hurls 5 rocks in a target direction, each dealing 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 ( +50% AP ) magic damage in an area around the first enemy hit. This creates Worked Ground below Taliyah. Subsequent hits against the same target deal 60% less damage. Threaded Volley casts on Worked Ground : Costs 10 Mana and consume the Worked Ground ; Have 30% less cooldown; Hurl a boulders that deals 100 / 140 / 180 / 220 ( +80% AP ) magic damage to the primary target and shows enemies hit by 25% / 30% / 35% / 40% for 1.5 seconds; Monsters hit by the boulders are stunned for 3 seconds. Each rock deals 20 bonus magic damage against monsters, and each boulders deals 36 bonus magic damage against monsters.

### [2] Seismic Shove

**Cooldown:** 13.0 / 11.0 / 9.0 / 7.0 seconds

**Cost:** 30.0 / 30.0 / 30.0 / 30.0

Causes an area of ground to erupt, knocking enemies in the area in the chosen direction after a brief delay.

### [3] Unraveled Earth

**Cooldown:** 12.0 / 12.0 / 12.0 / 12.0 seconds

**Cost:** 90.0 / 90.0 / 90.0 / 90.0

Scatters loose stones, dealing 65 / 120 / 175 / 230 ( +55% AP ) magic damage to enemies hit and slowing them by 20% for 0.5 seconds. The stones detonate when enemies dash or are knocked through them, dealing 20 / 35 / 50 / 65 ( +30% AP ) magic damage and stuns for the remaining movement duration + 0.75 seconds. Monsters are always stunned for the maximum duration. Deals 190% damage against monsters.

### [4] Weaver's Wall

**Cooldown:** 94.0 / 85.0 / 75.0 seconds

**Cost:** 100.0 / 100.0 / 100.0

Creates a massive earthen wall for 4 seconds : The wall knocks back nearby enemies when it forms; Once the wall is fully formed, Taliyah can recast to destroy it early. I f recast immediately : Taliyah rides along the wall as it unravels. Moving or being immobilized ends the ride. This ability cannon be cast if Taliyah has taken damage from champions or structures within the last 3 seconds.
