## Abilities

### [P] Vow of the First Lands

Critical Strikes deal an additional 10% (per 100 AP ) magic damage .

### [1] Cultivation of Spirit

**Cooldown:** 0.0 / 0.0 / 0.0 seconds

**Cost:** 30.0 / 30.0 / 30.0 / 30.0

S pirit Charge (Passive) : Attacks deal an additional 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 ( +20% AP ) magic damage ; When not active, launching an attack grants 1 Unleash. Attacking again refreshes Unleash's duration. Max Unleash is 6. If the duration is not refreshed, Yunara loses 1 Unleash every 0.5 seconds. Spirit Unbound (Active) : On cast, consumes Unleash, gaining 22% / 35% / 47% / 60% Attack Speed for 5 seconds. Attacks are empowered during this time to: Deal an additional 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 ( +20% AP ) magic damage on-hit; Spread to enemies nearby the target, dealing them 18 ( 30% AD ) physical damage . Transcendent State : This ability automatically activates until Yunara exits the state. If the attack Critically Strikes, its spread damage will also Critically Strike. Damage from spread attacks is increased to 250% against minions below 30% Health.

### [2] Arc of Judgment / Arc of Ruin

**Cooldown:** 10.0 / 10.0 / 10.0 / 10.0 seconds

**Cost:** 60.0 / 60.0 / 60.0 / 60.0

Arc of Judgment: Lets rip a spinning prayer bead in the target location, briefly granting vision of the area it travels through. The prayer bead slows enemies hit by 99% , lingering as it travels and resetting its remaining duration. When it reaches its endpoint, it lingers and expands for 1 second, regardless of whether it has hit an enemy. The initial hit deals 60 / 110 / 160 / 210 ( +85% bonus AD +50% AP ) magic damage and slows by 99% , decaying over 1.5 seconds; When lingering, the bead deals an additional 8 / 14 / 20 / 26 ( +12% bonus AD +7.5% AP ) magic damage to nearby enemies every 0.25 seconds. While Yunara is in a Transcendent State, this ability is upgraded to Arc of Ruin. Arc of Ruin : Fires a laser in the target direction that deals 160 ( +120% bonus AD +75% AP ) magic damage and slows by 99% , decaying over 1.5 seconds. Arc of Judgment deals 50% - 100% damage to minions and executes low-Health minions.

### [3] Kanmei's Steps / Untouchable Shadow

**Cooldown:** 9.0 / 9.0 / 9.0 / 9.0 seconds

**Cost:** 40.0 / 40.0 / 40.0 / 40.0

Kanmei's Steps : Gains 30% / 35% / 40% / 45% Movement Speed for 1.5 seconds, increased to 45% / 52.5% / 60% / 67.5% when moving toward a visible enemy champion nearby. While Yunara is in a Transcendent State, this ability is upgraded to Untouchable Shadow. Untouchable Shadow : Dashes in the target direction.

### [4] Transcend One's Self

**Cooldown:** 70.0 / 60.0 / 50.0 seconds

**Cost:** 100.0 / 100.0 / 100.0

Passive : Arc of Ruin's base damage and Untouchable Shadow's dash speed scale with this ability's level. Transcendent State (Active) : Enters a Transcendent State for 15 seconds. During this time, Yunara's basic abilities are empowered and cost no Mana. Cultivation of Spirit : Activates automatically for the duration. Arc of Judgment : Upgrades to Arc of Ruin . Its remaining cooldown reduces by 80% when Yunara enters or exits a Transcendent State. Kanmei's Steps : Upgrades to Untouchable Shadow . Its cooldown refreshes when Yunara enters or exits a Transcendent State.
