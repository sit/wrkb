# Death's Dance

**Type:** Defense,Fighter  
**Cost:** 3300 gold

## Stats

- +45 Armor
- +50 Attack Damage
- +15 Ability Haste

## Effects

UNIQUE - Cauterize: Store 30% of physical and magic damage taken, or 12% for ranged champions, and take the stored amount as true damage over 3 seconds.
UNIQUE - Defy: If a champion you damaged within the last 3 seconds dies, the remaining Ignore Pain damage is cleansed, and you heal over 2 seconds for an amount equal to 90% bonus Attack Damage.
