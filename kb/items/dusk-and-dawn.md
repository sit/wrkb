# Dusk and Dawn

**Type:** Magic  
**Cost:** 3100 gold

## Stats

- +300 Health
- +20% Attack Speed
- +60 Ability Power
- +20 Ability Haste

## Effects

SPELLBLADE - After casting an ability, your next attack deals bonus magic damage equal to 75% base Attack Damage + 10% Ability Power and heals you for 10% Ability Power + 3% bonus Health. Shortly afterward, it applies one additional on-hit effect to the target. (1.5 second cooldown) Deals reduced damage to structures.
