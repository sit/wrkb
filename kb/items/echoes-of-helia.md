# Echoes of Helia

**Type:** Support  
**Cost:** 2400 gold

## Stats

- +200 Health
- +50% Base Mana Regen
- +40 Ability Power
- +20 Ability Haste

## Effects

SOUL SIPHON: 30% of pre-mitigation damage dealt to enemy champions is stored as Soul Fragments, with a maximum of 80 - 250 based on level. When you heal or shield an allied champion other than yourself, all Soul Fragments are consumed to heal that target for an equal amount.
