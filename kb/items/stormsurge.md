# Stormsurge

**Type:** Magic  
**Cost:** 2900 gold

## Stats

- +90 Ability Power
- +15 Magic Penetration
- +6 Movement Speed

## Effects

STORMRAIDER - When you deal damage equal to 25% of a champion's max Health within 2.5 seconds, inflict Squall on them and gain 25% Movement Speed for 2.5 seconds. (25-second cooldown)
SQUALL - After 2 seconds, deal 125 + 10% Ability Power magic damage. If the target dies before Squall triggers, it instead deals the same amount of damage to nearby enemies and grants 25 gold.
