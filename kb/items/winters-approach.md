# Winter's Approach

**Type:** Defense  
**Cost:** 2600 gold

## Stats

- +500 Health
- +500 Mana
- +10 Ability Haste

## Effects

UNIQUE - AWE: Gain bonus Health equal to 15% of max Mana and refund 15% of total Mana spent.

UNIQUE - MANA CHARGE: Basic attacks, Mana expenditure, and taking damage from champions, structures, and epic monsters grant 14 max Mana. This effect grants up to 700 max Mana and can trigger at most 3 times every 10 seconds.

Transforms into Fimbulwinter at +700 mana.

Limited to 1 AWE item.
