# Dark Harvest

**Type:** Keystone
**Family:** Unknown

## Description

Damaging a champion below 50% Health deals adaptive damage and harvests their soul, permanently increasing Dark Harvest's damage by 10. (20 second cooldown. Resets to 1 second on takedown.)Dark Harvest Damage: 35 + 11 per soul (+10% bonus AD +5% bonus AP)
