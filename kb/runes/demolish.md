# Demolish

**Type:** Minor
**Family:** Resolve

## Description

When within 550 range of an enemy turret, gain a charge every 0.5s, up to 6 times. When fully charged, your next attack against the turret deals an additional 100 + 22% Max HP physical damage. (30s cooldown)
