# Empowerment

**Type:** Keystone
**Family:** Unknown

## Description

Hitting an enemy champion with 3 consecutive attacks deals 40 - 165 bonus adaptive damage (based on level) and increases your damage dealt by 8%, lasting until you exit combat with champions.
