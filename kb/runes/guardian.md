# Guardian

**Type:** Keystone
**Family:** Unknown

## Description

Guard allies within 350 units of you and allies you target with abilities for 2.5s. While guarding, if you or the ally take more than a certain amount of damage, both of you gain a shield of (45 - 165 + 6% bonus Health + 15% Ability Power) for 2s, cooldown: 55s - 25s.
