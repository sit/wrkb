# Chilling Smite

**Cooldown:** 15 seconds  
**Charges:** 2 (90 second recharge)  
**Range:** 500  
**Maps:** Summoner's Rift

## Description

Deal 1000 true damage to the target large or epic monster or minion and heal for 70 + (10% maximum health).

Chilling Smite can be cast on enemy champions, dealing 40 true damage and slowing the target by 25% for 2 seconds, while also granting you 25% bonus movement speed for the same duration.

Passive - Jungle Expertise:
- Earn 20% bonus experience and gold from monsters; bonus gold is disabled after 11:00 elapse. Earn 60% reduced experience and gold from minions until 06:00 elapse.
- Against monsters, your attacks deal 15% bonus physical damage, and your abilities deal 30% bonus physical damage.
- Attacking monsters restores 40 health over 5 seconds.
- Gain 20 mana regen while in jungle or river.

Passive - Conservation: Generate 6 stacks every 5 seconds, up to 180 stacks. When you kill a large or epic monster, consume all stacks to gain 1 gold per stack. Generation increases to 12 stacks per 5 seconds if your team's total gold is lower than the enemy by at least 10%, and your total gold is lower than your allies' average total gold by at least 5%.
