# Cleanse

**Cooldown:** 110 seconds  
**Range:** Self  
**Maps:** Summoner's Rift, Howling Abyss

## Description

Removes all disables (excluding Nearsight, and Suppression) and spell debuffs (excluding Mark) affecting your champion, and grants Crowd control immunity for 0.25 seconds.
