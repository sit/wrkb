# Execute Smite

**Range:** 500  
**Maps:** Summoner's Rift

## Description

Passive: If you would damage an epic monster within 800 health, or a large monster within 400 health, you deal true damage equal to that amount and heal for 8% maximum health. This will not trigger from persistent damage.

Passive - Jungle Expertise:
- Earn 20% bonus experience and gold from monsters. Bonus gold is disabled after 7:30. Earn 60% reduced experience and gold from minions until 07:00 elapse.
- Against monsters, your attacks deal 15% bonus physical damage, and your abilities deal 30% bonus physical damage. Bonus attack damage decays from decays from 15% - 0% (based on game time).
- Attacking monsters restores 40 health over 5 seconds.
- Gain 20 mana regen while in jungle or river.

Passive - Conservation: Generate 6 stacks per 5 seconds. When you kill a large or epic monster, consume all stacks to gain 1 gold per stack. Generation increases to 12 stacks per 5 seconds if your team's total gold is lower than the enemy by at least 10%, and your total gold is lower than your allies' average total gold by at least 5%.
