# Exhaust

**Cooldown:** 100 seconds  
**Range:** 600  
**Maps:** Summoner's Rift, Howling Abyss

## Description

Exhaust the target enemy champion for 2.5 seconds, reducing their damage dealt by 40% and slowing them by 35% decaying over the duration.
