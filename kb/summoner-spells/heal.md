# Heal

**Cooldown:** 100 seconds  
**Range:** 850  
**Maps:** Summoner's Rift, Howling Abyss

## Description

Heal for 110 - 400 (based on level) to you and the most wounded nearby ally champion, and both of you gain 30% bonus movement speed for 1 second.

Champions affected by Heal within 35 seconds will receive 50% heal amount.
