# Ignite

**Cooldown:** 100 seconds  
**Range:** 600  
**Maps:** Summoner's Rift, Howling Abyss

## Description

Ignite the target enemy champion for 5 seconds, revealing them, inflicting 60% Grievous Wounds, and dealing 72 - 380 (based on level) true damage over the duration.

The first tick of damage occurs briefly after the cast, then subsequent ticks occur every 1.056 seconds.
